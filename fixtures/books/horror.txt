Ada returned to Lastlight Ward with a hearing trumpet and a lantern she kept hooded. In the house above the orchard, the barometer never fell even when rain broke the windows. Bess had stayed to listen to those nobody else could bear to hear. Now their names were fading from the ward book. The rooms were warm because a keeper refused to let grief step outside. Ada did not come to defeat a monster with a sword. She came to witness the absences, to share the weight of a name, and to find a door that could open without forgetting.
